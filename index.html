Disguise mode

When on, the app opens with a fake “Reroll has stopped working” message instead of the PIN screen. Tap the message text twice, then tap OK to reach the PIN screen. Tapping OK any other way shows a black screen, as if the app had closed. It applies on launch, when you tap Lock, and when auto-lock kicks in.

This is a disguise on top of your PIN, not extra encryption. The PIN is still required.

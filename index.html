LOW RISK

Informational events. This does not guarantee that all account activity is safe.

Disguise mode

When on, the app opens with a fake “Reroll has stopped working” message instead of the PIN screen. Tap the message text twice, then tap OK to reach the PIN screen. Tapping OK any other way opens a fake Error 404 page, as if the app had crashed. It applies on launch, when you tap Lock, and when auto-lock kicks in.

This is a disguise on top of your PIN, not extra encryption. The PIN is still required.
